Demand and supply of oil

Interactive oil market game · build a four-step demand and supply story

Oil has few substitutes, so demand is inelastic and the demand curve is steep. A supply shift therefore changes the price a lot and the quantity only a little.

Build one oil-market story in four steps. In each step, pick a cause, pick what happens, then add it. The diagram keeps the old equilibrium and shows excess demand or excess supply at the old price. Then show the price moving to the new equilibrium.

Words used on the diagrams
  • Inelastic demand — buyers change the quantity only a little when the price changes, so D is steep.
  • Shift — the whole curve moves. A movement along a curve is different: only the price has changed.
  • Excess demand — at the current price, buyers want more than sellers offer. The price rises.
  • Excess supply — at the current price, sellers offer more than buyers want. The price falls.
  • Extension — quantity rises as the market moves along a curve. Contraction — quantity falls as the market moves along a curve.
